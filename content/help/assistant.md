# The LearnProof Assistant

## What it answers
The assistant answers questions about LearnProof from LearnProof's own help, the catalog of niches, stages, and resources, and, when you ask about yourself, your own progress. It cites its sources and links to them. If its sources do not cover a question, it says it is not sure and points to the closest page.

## Hint mode
The assistant never does your explain-back for you. If your question names an idea from a stage you have not passed yet, however you word it, it switches to hint mode: it points to the stage's resources, asks a guiding question or two, and offers a hint. In hint mode it also leaves out the earlier turns of the chat. After you pass the stage, it explains normally.

## Limits and privacy
Open it with Ctrl or Cmd + J, the Ask LearnProof button in the top bar, or search. Each question counts toward the same AI limits as explain-back. Chats are kept in the open page only and never saved.
