# The SkillFlow Assistant

## What it answers
The assistant answers questions about SkillFlow from SkillFlow's own help, the catalog of niches, stages, and resources, and, when you ask about yourself, your own progress. It cites its sources and links to them. If its sources do not cover a question, it says it is not sure and points to the closest page.

## Hint mode
The assistant never does your explain-back for you. If you ask it to explain an idea from a stage you have not passed yet, it switches to hint mode: it points to the stage's resources, asks a guiding question or two, and offers a hint. After you pass the stage, it explains normally.

## Limits and privacy
Open it with Ctrl or Cmd + J, the Ask SkillFlow button in the top bar, or search. Each question counts toward the same AI limits as explain-back. Chats are kept in the open page only and never saved.
