# Career-fit test

## What it is
The career-fit test suggests free paths that fit your interests and traits. It uses two published instruments with their item text unchanged: the IPIP-NEO-120 personality inventory (public domain) and the O*NET Interest Profiler Short Form (USDOL/ETA, CC BY 4.0). It has 18 pages of ten questions, saved after every page. Home shows a card that invites you, resumes the test, or opens your report.

## How paths are matched
Path fit is SkillFlow's own map from each free path to three interest areas and helpful traits, weighted 80 percent interest and 20 percent traits. Scores are never described as percentiles or compared with a population. The report can be downloaded as an image.

## What it is not
It is self-discovery, not a clinical assessment or career counselling. Retake and Delete my results both delete your answers.
