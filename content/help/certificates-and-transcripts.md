# Certificates and transcripts

## Transcripts
A transcript lists every stage you have passed on a path, with the real dates, and downloads as a PDF. Progress links to it after your first pass on a followed path.

## Certificates
A certificate appears only when every stage on a path is passed. A free path can earn one. It is SkillFlow's own record of what you explained, and it is not a license or a degree. Progress shows your records as cards with Transcript or Certificate buttons.
