# Creator studio

## Uploading a video
Creator studio is where you upload a video you own, keep the draft, and send it for review. After an admin approves it, it goes live in that niche's clip feed and on your public profile.

## Your numbers
The studio shows your views, watch time, and how many plays reached most of the video. Those numbers stay off the feed and off your profile. Replacing the file on a live video sends it back to review. There are no creator ratings or earnings.
