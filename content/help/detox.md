# Dopamine Detox and the Detox Score

## The self-check
Dopamine Detox starts with a 20-question self-check about screen time, short video and social habits, reaching for your phone, focus, sleep and routine, offline interests, and focus and mood. It gives your starting score. It is a self-improvement score from your own answers and your learning activity, not a medical test.

## How the Detox Score is calculated
The score runs from -100 to +100. Your self-check answers set the baseline. After that, only verified learning since the baseline moves it: each idea passed adds 1, each stage passed 2, a recall adds 1 when strong, 0.5 when partial and 0.2 when weak, a practice note 0.5, an Open Source contribution 1, focus sessions 1 per 25 focused minutes (from 10 minutes), and 0.5 for each active day. A single day can add at most 5 points, so hours alone never inflate it. No model ever sets the number.

## Idle weeks and check-ins
After every 7 days without activity, earned progress eases back by 10 percent, never below your baseline. The weekly check-in (screen time and focus) can nudge the score by at most 10 either way. Every change is explained in words, such as "+6 this week: 9 ideas passed".

## Bands
Overstimulated is below -30, Rebalancing is from -30, Steady is from 10, and Deep focus is from 50.

## Focus sessions and Swap a scroll
A focus session lasts 25 or 50 minutes and counts only while a lesson, an explain-back, Notes, or a recall card is open in a visible tab. Swap a scroll is a 3-minute dialog for one due recall or the next unexplained idea. Retake or Delete my results removes the assessment, check-ins, and focus sessions. The score shows on your profile only if you switch it on.
