# Explain-back

## What explain-back is
Explain-back is the only mastery check in SkillFlow. There is no quiz. After you work through a stage's resources, you explain the stage's ideas in your own words, one idea at a time. A stage is recorded only after every idea has passed.

## How an idea passes
Each idea is sent to the review model on its own. An idea holds when you explain what it is, and why it matters or when you would use it, in your own words. Naming the idea, a single clause, or a wrong or confused explanation does not pass. Every answer gets a written review back, whether it holds or not.

## When an answer is thin
An empty answer stays on the step. A wrong or thin answer also stays on the step and shows a written review that says what is missing. You can try that idea again as many times as you like. Nothing lets you skip an idea.

## What happens when every idea holds
Each idea that holds is saved to Notes with your explanation and its review. When the last idea holds, the stage is recorded as passed and the next stage on the path opens.

## Where the ideas come from
The ideas are written from the stage's own resources the first time the gate opens, and again when those resources change. Every key point from the resources is kept. Explain-back runs in focus mode, which hides the sidebar, and your draft is kept in this browser until the stage is saved.

## When the review cannot run
If the model cannot be reached, times out, or replies with something that is not a review, nothing is saved and you can try again.
