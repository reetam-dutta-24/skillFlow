# Personal learning plan

## What a plan does
Every free path can have one personal plan. You set your preferences on that path's page, and plain code builds a weekly schedule from the path's stages. The path page then shows the weeks and links each one into its stage. Saving a plan also follows that path.

## Changing or undoing a plan
Pace does not decide which stages are open: the explain-back still opens each stage. Undo on the path page hides your schedule and shows the shared stages again; it does not change the stages or their resources. While a plan is on, a stage lists your selected resources in order and marks the extras Optional.
