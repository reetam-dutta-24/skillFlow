# Mastery, streaks, and weak topics

## Mastery
Mastery for a path is the share of its stages with a passed explain-back. A passed stage counts as 100 on that path's lane on Home. With no explain-back attempts yet, every number is zero.

## Streaks
A streak day is a UTC day on which you saved an explain-back or a note. Your current streak stays alive through today and yesterday; a gap resets it to 0. Your longest streak is kept. The bell can remind you when yesterday counted and today has not yet.

## Topics to strengthen
Weak topics come from what the explain-back review wrote back. An idea you have not passed yet ranks first and links to the explain-back. An idea that took two or more misses before it passed, or whose latest recall was rated weak or partial, ranks after it and links to the lesson. One slip that later passed is not weak, and a strong recall clears an earlier weak one. Home shows four; Progress and Analytics use the same list.
