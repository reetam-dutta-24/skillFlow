# Nearby events and the learner map

## The learner map
The learner map on Nearby shows how many learners study near each city. It never shows names or one pin per person, and it never reads GPS. Cities with too few learners are left off. Saving your city in Settings is free. Showing yourself on the map is part of Premium.

## Nearby events
Nearby events lists meetups, workshops, and online events for your niches near your saved city, with a map, filters for niche, date (this week, this month, next 3 months), and distance (25 to 200 km). Each card links to the event; there is no booking or RSVP. Without a saved city you still see online events.

## Community events
Any signed-in learner can submit a community event, at most 3 a day. It appears after an admin or that niche's reviewer approves it. Live search with any keyword and city is part of Premium.
