# Notes and export

## What goes into Notes
Every idea you explain well in an explain-back is saved to Notes: your explanation and its written review. Notes are personal and only you see them. Notes is in the sidebar, and each stage shows its photo.

## Export and summaries
Notes can be downloaded as a Word document or a PDF. A stage can also be summarised from your notes alone. Notes are not turned into lessons.
