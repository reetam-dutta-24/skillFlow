# Open Source community

## What a contribution is
Open Source is where learners share what they know with a niche: text, an image, and source links. A moderator publishes a contribution, and only then is it public. Coming-soon niches have communities too.

## Writing and editing
Write a contribution from a niche's Open Source page. You can edit a contribution while it waits for review. Editing a published one takes it off the public list and sends it back to review. Withdraw closes a waiting one. My contributions lists yours and their reviews.

## Questions and reviews
A published post takes one question per learner, and the author writes one answer. There is no thread, no likes, and no view count. Admins review everywhere; a maintainer or reviewer reviews one niche. Nobody reviews their own contribution. A rejection comes with a reason and a note.
