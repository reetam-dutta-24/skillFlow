# Practice notes and your own material

## Practice notes
Practice notes grade an idea against material you bring: a link to a public page, a dropped text file, or pasted text. Only that text is used as the source. When your explanation holds, a practice note is saved to Notes.

## What practice does not do
A practice note does not pass a stage, does not add a resource to the catalog, and is not seen by anyone else.

## Bring your own resource
Bring your own resource (from Submit a resource) gives you an explain-back prompt for a resource you found yourself.
