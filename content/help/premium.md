# Free and Premium

## What is free
Thirty paths are free, every stage included, so finishing a free path earns its certificate. Explain-back, notes, recall, progress, the learner map itself, nearby events, and certificates are free.

## What Premium adds
Premium is one Stripe subscription. It opens the niches outside the thirty free paths, lets you appear on the learner map, and adds live event search. A Premium niche shows a Premium label; a free account opens the upgrade page from it. Premium niches have no stages yet.

## Price and billing
The current price is shown on the upgrade page and at Stripe Checkout; the card form stays on Stripe. Premium counts while the subscription is active or trialing. Manage or cancel it from the upgrade page through the Stripe customer portal.
