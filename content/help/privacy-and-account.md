# Privacy and deleting your account

## What is personal
Your progress, notes, plan, Detox Score, and career-fit results are personal. Assistant chats are not stored in the database; they live in the open page and disappear when you reload or clear them. Your profile shows your activity heatmap only while Show activity is on, and your Detox Score only if you switch it on.

## Deleting your account
Delete your account yourself in Settings, Danger zone. You type DELETE, and your password if the account has one. An active Premium subscription is cancelled before anything is deleted. Your stage completions, contributions, submitted events, uploaded files, and the account itself are removed. The Privacy Policy and Terms describe exactly this.
