# Recall

## How recall works
A stage idea you passed comes back twice, 18 hours apart, as a short card over the app. The card stays until you write an answer. A thin answer still lets you back into the app.

## How a recall is graded
Each recall answer is rated strong, partial, or weak, and always gets a written review. A partial or weak answer gets the idea explained in plain language, so you leave knowing it. Recall scores feed Retention on Progress. A weak recall can bring the idea back into Topics to strengthen. Your stage pass always stays.
