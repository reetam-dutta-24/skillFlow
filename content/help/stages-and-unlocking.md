# Paths, stages, and how stages unlock

## Paths and stages
A path is an ordered list of stages for one niche. A stage gathers real external resources: videos, articles and docs, and courses. The Paths page lists the paths you follow; each path page shows every stage with a photo, a state chip (passed, up next, or locked), and the reason it is locked.

## Why a stage is locked
The next stage opens only after you pass the explain-back on the stage before it. A locked stage says "Pass the previous stage to open this one." It still shows its title, photo, and description. A stage you already passed always stays open.

## Free paths
Thirty paths are free, and every stage on them opens to you in order. Passing every stage on a free path earns its certificate.

## Lessons
Opening a stage starts a step-by-step lesson: an intro, "Watch first" when there is a clip, one step per resource, and a last step that leads to Explain it back. Videos play inside the app with the privacy-enhanced YouTube player; docs and courses open in a new tab. Your step is remembered per stage in this browser.
