# Themes, accents, and settings

## Themes and accents
Settings, Appearance has light and dark themes, ten colour presets, Spectrum (a slow drift around the colour wheel that holds one hue under reduced motion), and a custom gradient with a palette, hex, and RGB fields. Your choice applies immediately and is saved to your account.

## Profile and notifications
Your display name (2 to 50 characters, never an email address) and photo (an upload or a generated avatar) are set in Settings, Profile. Settings, Notifications has a switch for reviews, community activity, the Detox check-in, and the streak reminder.

## Search and the guided tour
Press Ctrl or Cmd + K, or "/", to search pages, features, settings, niches, stages, and resources. The guided tour on Home walks through every feature with something small to try; replay it from Home or from search.
